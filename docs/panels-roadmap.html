  • Pure black ground, dark grey cards at a 14–20px radius, pill buttons.
  • Lime only for the primary action and the key number. AVEX uses it sparingly but decisively — keep that discipline.
  • Monospace for every number and identifier — balances, prices, addresses, invoice IDs. AVEX already does this (#isaac, Elo 19865, 1,948 AX).
  • Emoji as section markers — part of the AVEX voice, not decoration.
  • One dark theme. AVEX has no light theme; building one for Pay would separate it from the family.

🛡️ Admin panel — ours

This is the AVEX team's tool, not the merchant's. The test for Tier 1 was simple: without it, resolving a problem requires psql — and in a money system, hand access to the database is both dangerous and unauditable.

Tier 1 Cannot operate the system without these 3–4 weeks
  1. 01
    Merchant list and detail
    KYB state, risk tier, volume, enabled networks, suspend and reinstate. Suspension must take effect immediately and stop new invoices being issued.
    foundation
  2. 02
    Contract review queue
    The phase-3 probe result with every finding, and an approve-or-reject decision with a note. Right now the only way to change a verdict is to edit the database.
    phase 3 ready
  3. 03
    Settlement monitor
    Queue depth, current gas, deferrals, stuck transactions, and gas wallet balance and runway. The runner already produces these figures; they have nowhere to be seen.
    phase 4 ready
  4. 04
    Unmatched payment reconciliation
    TON without a memo, wrong amount, wrong asset. Manual assignment to an invoice, or a return path. Every unmatched payment is an angry customer, and without this tool each one takes hours.
    critical
  5. 05
    System health
    Per-network RPC state, open oracle breakers, per-network watcher lag, webhook error rate. Watcher lag means payments are not being seen — and it happens silently.
    critical
  6. 06
    Audit log search
    Across all merchants, filtered by actor, action and target. The table has existed since phase 1 and is being written to; nothing reads it.
    phase 1 ready
Tier 2 Needed as volume grows 3–4 weeks
  1. 07
    Daily financial reconciliation
    On-chain against the database, per network, per day, with a drift alarm. This is the only thing that says the books are right — and without it a mistake stays hidden for months.
    important
  2. 08
    Global invoice and payment search
    The support tool. By transaction hash, address, identifier or amount.
    support
  3. 09
    Refund approval
    A crypto refund has to go to the sending address — and if that is an exchange's hot wallet, the money is lost. Exchange-address detection and a mandatory destination confirmation.
    dangerous
  4. 10
    Global asset list management
    Add a curated asset, flag risk, force fixed_rate. The list currently lives in code, so changing it needs a release.
    operational
  5. 11
    Per-merchant pricing
    A negotiated rate for a high-volume merchant, with an enforced floor. A prerequisite for enterprise sales.
    commercial
  6. 12
    Support view (impersonation)
    Seeing a merchant's dashboard to resolve a problem — read-only and fully in the audit log. Without both constraints it becomes an attack vector itself.
    handle with care
  7. 13
    Webhook delivery inspector
    Per-merchant history, failure reason, and replay. The service and its table are ready from phase 4.
    phase 4 ready
Tier 3 Scale and operations 4–5 weeks
  1. 14
    Gas wallet management
    Hot and warm separation, per-network spend cap, top-up request. The runner enforces the cap; setting it should not need a deploy.
    operational
  2. 15
    Manual breaker control
    Open or close a network or an asset by hand. When something looks wrong, an operator must be able to stop it immediately, without a release.
    safety
  3. 16
    Alert routing
    Who gets paged for what. Alerts are produced in phases 2 and 4 and currently only sit in the log.
    operational
  4. 17
    Merchant onboarding flow
    Approve a signup, request documents, assign a tier. While onboarding is manual, this queue is what stops applications getting lost.
    growth
  5. 18
    Reporting and export
    Volume, revenue, per-network economics, CSV export for accounting.
    commercial
  6. 19
    Per-merchant feature flags
    Access to a new network, beta features, a volume ceiling during beta.
    growth
  7. 20
    Stars and Fragment reconciliation
    Stars balance, the 21-day lock, withdrawal tracking. The Stars path has its own lifecycle and needs its own view.
    phase 10

🤝 Reseller panel

A reseller brings merchants and earns commission. The whole value of this panel is one thing: a reseller must be able to see their own earnings and prove them — otherwise they message support every month and trust erodes.

Tier 1 Minimum shippable 2–3 weeks
  1. 01
    Referral link and code, with attribution
    Attribution must be fixed at signup and immutable thereafter — reassignable attribution means arguments over commission.
    foundation
  2. 02
    Their own merchant list
    Status and volume — without seeing those merchants' invoices or customers. A reseller has no right to a merchant's commercial data.
    boundary
  3. 03
    Commission dashboard
    Earned, pending, paid — broken down by merchant and period, with an auditable calculation basis. A number with no explanation generates a ticket.
    heart of the panel
  4. 04
    Payout request and history
    Minimum amount, waiting period, receiving address. Changing a reseller's receiving address must carry the same 24-hour delay — it is the same attack vector.
    security
Tier 2 Growing the sales network 2–3 weeks
  1. 05
    Volume-based commission tiers
    Show the current tier and the distance to the next. Real motivation, and consistent with AVEX's own Elo and Rank culture.
    motivational
  2. 06
    Signing up a merchant on their behalf
    Pre-fill the form and send an invitation. The merchant must set their own password and second factor — a reseller must never have access to a merchant account.
    boundary
  3. 07
    Marketing kit
    Banners, prepared copy, an integration guide in Persian, video. The Iranian market moves on Persian-language content.
    sales
  4. 08
    View of their merchants' tickets
    Read-only, with escalation to AVEX. The reseller is first-line support.
    support
  5. 09
    Read-only reseller API key
    For their own dashboard. Scoped to aggregate statistics for their own merchants only — no invoices, no customers.
    technical
Tier 3 Optional, once the model is proven 3–4 weeks
  1. 10
    White-labelled checkout
    The reseller's logo on their merchants' checkout. Opens up enterprise sales.
    commercial
  2. 11
    Pricing within an allowed band
    The reseller sets their merchant's rate within a band AVEX defines; the difference is their commission.
    commercial
  3. 12
    Reseller leaderboard
    Fits AVEX's gamification culture (Elo, Rank, Streak) exactly, and creates healthy competition.
    motivational
  4. 13
    Training and certification
    A reseller who does not understand the product brings difficult merchants and raises the support load.
    quality
  5. 14
    Sub-resellers — two levels maximum
    If it becomes necessary, cap it at two levels. Deeper than that the structure resembles multi-level marketing, which risks both the brand and access to the infrastructure.
    handle with care
▲ One serious point about commission

Commission charged on payment volume is treated as a regulated "payment agent" model in many jurisdictions. If you ever take merchants outside Iran, this structure is the first thing that gets examined. Keep it to the domestic market for now, and keep the calculation basis transparent and auditable from day one — reconstructing commission history after the fact is close to impossible.

🔑 API, keys and documentation

The key infrastructure has been ready since phase 1 — scoped keys, test and live modes, hashed storage, and a prohibition on headless keys holding elevation-gated scopes. What remains is the public API surface and the docs.

■ The admin panel must not share an origin with the merchant dashboard

Separate subdomain, IP allowlist, separate cookie. On the same origin, an XSS in the merchant dashboard reaches the admin panel too — and the admin panel can suspend merchants and change contract verdicts.

  • Headers: Authorization: Bearer ak_live_… · Idempotency-Key · AVEX-Version for versioning
  • Errors: one fixed shape with error, message and fields — parseable, and safe to show an end user
  • Test mode: an ak_test_ key must drive a fake chain so a merchant can exercise the whole cycle without spending money. Without it, every merchant's first test is with real funds.

Documentation — in order of importance

  • Five-minute quickstart — from key to first test invoice. If this section does not work, nothing else gets read.
  • Webhook verification with runnable code in Node, PHP and Python. Most integration bugs live here.
  • Invoice lifecycle with a state diagram, including underpaid, overpaid and reorg reversal.
  • Network guide — cost, speed, memo requirement and minimum amount per network.
  • WooCommerce plugin — the Iranian market is largely WordPress, which makes this more important than the SDKs.
  • SDKs for Node, PHP and Python, a Postman collection, and an error reference.
