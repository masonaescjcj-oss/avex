    💳 What you pay

    A percentage of what you process, and nothing else. No monthly fee, no minimum, and nothing to pay in a month you took no payments. It comes out of each payment on chain as it is settled to you, so there is never an invoice from us.

    🧮 Who pays it

    Our cut is taken on chain, out of the payment, in the same transaction that pays you. So passing it on does not bill your customer separately — it asks them for enough that what reaches you is your full price. Either way we receive the same, so this is your pricing decision and not ours.
