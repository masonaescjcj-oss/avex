    Currencies

    Turn on the currencies you want to take. Each one needs a payout address on its chain — the money goes straight to your own wallet, so without an address there is nowhere for it to go and invoices are refused.

    What you accept

    Your own token

    Issued your own token? Submit its contract and we will check it. Approval is not automatic and not instant: the whole defence against a token calling itself USDT is that somebody looks.

    Only EVM chains for now — TON, TRON and Solana contracts need a different probe, and submitting one we cannot read would tell you nothing.

    Payouts

    Where the money lands: your own wallets on each chain, and the addresses we settle to.

    Your own wallets

    Your customers pay straight into a wallet of yours, on any chain — no contract of ours in the path, nothing to sweep, and no network fee for us to pass on. Register one to ten addresses per chain and we hand them out. Each invoice asks for a very slightly different amount — 20.05, 20.03 — and that is how two payments to one address are told apart. Paying into your own wallet has no minimum order size.

    Your first wallet on a chain is usable at once. Every one after it waits 24 hours and everyone in your organization is emailed, because these are addresses your money lands in — the same protection a payout address gets, for the same reason. Retiring one is immediate.

    Payout addresses

    A change here is scheduled rather than immediate, and we email you when it is requested. That delay is the difference between a stolen session costing you one order and costing you everything after it.

    Who can get in

    Only the owner can change where money is sent. That is not a setting — it is the one permission no admin has, so a compromised admin account cannot redirect your revenue.

    Security

    A second factor for anything that moves money or grants access, and the sessions that hold it.

    Two-factor authentication

    An authenticator app on your phone, and a six-digit code from it whenever something on this account moves money or grants access — a payout address, a live key, a new member. Your password alone cannot do those things, which is what makes a stolen password survivable.

    Scan this with Google Authenticator, Aegis, 1Password, or any app that takes a QR code. Or type the key in by hand:

    Other sessions

    Commission

    What you pay us, who pays it, and anything outstanding between us.

    Your balance

    What you pay

    A percentage of what you process, and nothing else. No monthly fee, no minimum, and nothing to pay in a month you took no payments. It comes out of each payment on chain as it is settled to you, so there is never an invoice from us.

    Who pays it

    Our cut is taken on chain, out of the payment, in the same transaction that pays you. So passing it on does not bill your customer separately — it asks them for enough that what reaches you is your full price. Either way we receive the same, so this is your pricing decision and not ours.
