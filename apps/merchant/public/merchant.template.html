    What you accept

    Telegram Stars

    not connected

    Two ways to take Stars. Your own bot can create the Telegram invoice and report the payment to us through the API — nothing here is needed for that. Or connect the bot below and Stars appear on your payment page beside your other currencies, and we do the talking.

    A bot has one webhook, so connecting it here takes over every update that bot receives. We answer the two payment updates and forward the rest to the URL you give — leave it empty only if this bot does nothing else. The Stars land in the bot's own Telegram balance either way: we are the record, never the custodian.

    Your own token

    Issued your own token? Submit its contract and we will check it. Approval is not automatic and not instant: the whole defence against a token calling itself USDT is that somebody looks.

    Only EVM chains for now — TON, TRON and Solana contracts need a different probe, and submitting one we cannot read would tell you nothing.

    Payouts

    Where the money lands: your own wallets on each chain, and the addresses we settle to.

    Your own wallets

    Your customers pay straight into a wallet of yours, on any chain — no contract of ours in the path, no gas, and a $0.50 minimum. Up to a hundred addresses per chain, and each invoice asks for a very slightly different amount — 20.001, 20.002 — which is how two payments to one address are told apart.

    Every wallet works at once, and everyone in your organization is emailed the moment one is added, because this is where your money lands. Retiring one is immediate. Wallets are used in turn: an idle one first, and only when every wallet has an open invoice do two invoices share one.

    Payout addresses

    A change here is scheduled rather than immediate, and we email you when it is requested. That delay is the difference between a stolen session costing you one order and costing you everything after it.

    Other sessions

    Commission

    What you pay us, who pays it, and anything outstanding between us.

    Your balance

    What you pay

    A percentage of what you process, and nothing else. No monthly fee, no minimum, and nothing to pay in a month you took no payments. It comes out of each payment on chain as it is settled to you, so there is never an invoice from us.

    Who pays it

    Our cut is taken on chain, out of the payment, in the same transaction that pays you. So passing it on does not bill your customer separately — it asks them for enough that what reaches you is your full price. Either way we receive the same, so this is your pricing decision and not ours.
