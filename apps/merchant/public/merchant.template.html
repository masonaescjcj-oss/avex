    🧾 Invoices

    💰 What you accept

    Turn on the currencies you want to take. Each one needs a payout address on its chain — the money goes straight to your own wallet, so without an address there is nowhere for it to go and invoices are refused.

    ➕ Your own token

    Issued your own token? Submit its contract and we will check it. Approval is not automatic and not instant: the whole defence against a token calling itself USDT is that somebody looks.

    Only EVM chains for now — TON, TRON and Solana contracts need a different probe, and submitting one we cannot read would tell you nothing.

    🏦 Payout addresses

    A change here is scheduled rather than immediate, and we email you when it is requested. That delay is the difference between a stolen session costing you one order and costing you everything after it.

    👛 Your own wallets on TRON

    On TRON your customers pay straight into a wallet of yours — no contract of ours in the path, nothing to sweep, and no network fee for us to pass on. Register three to five addresses and we hand them out; each invoice asks for a very slightly different amount, a fraction of a cent apart, and that is how two payments to one address are told apart.

    Your first wallet is usable at once. Every one after it waits 24 hours and everyone in your organization is emailed, because these are addresses your money lands in — the same protection a payout address gets, for the same reason. Retiring one is immediate.

    Your balance

    💳 What you pay

    A percentage of what you process, and nothing else. No monthly fee, no minimum, and nothing to pay in a month you took no payments. It comes out of each payment on chain as it is settled to you, so there is never an invoice from us.

    🧮 Who pays it

    Our cut is taken on chain, out of the payment, in the same transaction that pays you. So passing it on does not bill your customer separately — it asks them for enough that what reaches you is your full price. Either way we receive the same, so this is your pricing decision and not ours.
